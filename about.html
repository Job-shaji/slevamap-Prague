About SlevaMap Prague

A clearer way to find value in Prague.

SlevaMap Prague is a CZU student project that brings everyday and student offers together in one practical local guide. We want it to be easier to compare opportunities, understand who can use them, and find the original offer details before you go.

A university project, not a commercial company. Offers are provided by third parties and can change; always check the linked provider for current terms.

Explore Prague offers

Our purpose

Useful information, without the guesswork.

We organize offers by category and eligibility so Prague residents, visitors, and students can find options that suit them. Each listing should make its audience and redemption requirements easy to understand, with a direct link to the provider for the details that matter at checkout.

What guides us

Our values

  • 01

    Open to everyone

    Good value is not only for students. We include offers for the wider public and clearly mark when a discount does require student status or a membership account.

  • 02

    Clear and transparent

    Eligibility, important conditions, and provider links should be visible before you plan a purchase. We avoid presenting an offer as guaranteed when its terms can change.

  • 03

    Locally relevant

    We focus on Prague and useful options for life in the city, while identifying online or nationwide deals so you can judge whether they apply to you.

  • 04

    Responsible by design

    We link back to providers for the latest details and encourage visitors to check location, dates, exclusions, and redemption steps before relying on an offer.

A note about offers

SlevaMap is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with the merchants listed. Provider availability, prices, eligibility, and terms may change without notice. The merchant’s current terms always take precedence.
